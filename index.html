About the App

DV-Writing Assistant is a free writing companion built for Android phones. It helps you refine drafts, rephrase sentences, shorten long passages, fix grammar and cite sources without leaving your browser.

What you can do

  • Paraphrase: three alternative wordings of any passage.
  • Summarize: condense long text into a short, clear summary.
  • Check Grammar: mistakes highlighted inline with corrections.
  • Generate Citation: APA and MLA references from a DOI or book title.
  • Bibliography: collect, copy and export citations as plain text.

Built for real life

Drafts are saved on your own device. The interface works offline, and online tools say clearly when they need a connection.

Why I Built This

Good writing tools are often locked behind subscriptions, heavy desktop software or fast connections. Many students and professionals write mostly on a phone, with limited data. That gap is why this app exists.

The problem

Paraphrasing, grammar checking and citation formatting usually mean switching between three or four websites, each with its own limits and paywalls.

The goal

  • One simple screen for the everyday writing jobs.
  • Free tools wherever possible, with no hidden keys or accounts.
  • Fast on mid-range Android phones and usable offline.
  • Your text stays under your control.

How to Use

Write or paste

Type your draft, or tap Paste to bring in text you copied elsewhere. Import loads a .txt or .md file.

Pick a tool

  • Paraphraser: tap it, wait for three options, then tap Replace in Editor to use one.
  • Summarizer: works best on a paragraph or longer.
  • Grammar Checker: mistakes are highlighted, with a correction for each one.
  • Citation Gen: enter a DOI such as 10.1038/nature14539, or a book title, then copy the APA or MLA version.

Save and share

  • Tap Save File to store a draft on your device.
  • Open Saved Files from the right menu to continue later.
  • In Bibliography, remove entries or export everything as plain text.

Personalize

Open the three-dot menu to switch dark mode or change the text size.

Offline

Editing, saving and bibliography work without internet. Paraphrase, summarize, grammar and citation lookup need a connection.
